## Code of Conduct

**A respectful, welcoming community around ParseCore, on GitHub and on Discord.**

</div>

### Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of ParseCore pledge to make
participation in our project and our community an experience free from
harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

### Our standards

Behaviour that makes this community better:

* Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
* Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
* Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience.
* Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour we do not tolerate:

* Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing another person's private information without their explicit
  permission.
* Requests related to cheating, such as help with manipulating score
  submission. ParseCore is a calculation library, not a cheating tool.
* Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

### Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
will take fair, appropriate corrective action in response to any behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with
this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reasons for a moderation decision
when appropriate.

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces: the GitHub repository
(issues, pull requests, discussions), the
[Discord server](https://discord.gg/9p7whE7QxQ), and any other official channel.
It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public.

### Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the
maintainers at [conduct@olib.dev](mailto:conduct@olib.dev). Every report is
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of
the reporter is respected.

Maintainers follow this ladder of consequences:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning that explains the nature of the
   violation.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no
   interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction or public
   communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the
   community.

[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
